Chapter I — The Last Dreamer

Before the maps were drawn, before the first kings carved their names into stone, there was only the Dreamscape — a living world woven from the sleeping minds of every soul who ever closed their eyes. Every thought became a river. Every fear, a shadow. Every hope, a floating city of light.

But dreams cannot last forever. When the last true Dreamer vanished from the waking world, the Dreamscape began to crack. Forests turned to ash, moonlight dimmed, and the ancient seals that once held the darkness at bay faltered one by one.

Chapter II — The Three Seals

The elders spoke of three sacred seals — each guarding a fragment of the Dreamer's memory. Whosoever reawakens them shall restore the balance between worlds. But the path is not for the timid. Each seal tests the traveller: the Seal of Memory, the Seal of Courage, and the Seal of Truth.

Only one who carries a heart unbroken by fear may walk the fading paths and hear the whispers of the old world once more.

“The door only opens for those who dare to knock
with a heart that still remembers how to dream.”
